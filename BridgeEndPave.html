Instructions & calculation basis

Enter W1 and W2 in decimal feet, skew Z in degrees, and the rail height. Check the live quantities and plan dimensions. Add a record for each bridge end. In the SDBE-1A plan, the crown line divides W2 above and W1 below. Confirm widths and applicability against the project plans.

Save project downloads a JSON file containing all inputs. Keep it separately and use Open project to continue later. Save again after editing; closing this page does not save your work. Export text downloads a quantity report. Print / PDF opens the browser print dialog; choose Save as PDF for a portable report.

Uses the recovered MDOT Bridge End Pavement, Version AI 1.2 calculation: W = W1 + W2 + 1 ft with no rail, + 2.833333 ft for 32 in. rail, or + 3 ft for 42 in. rail. Side A = max(10, 20 − W tan Z / 2). Side B follows the corresponding original branch. Rail options include the original paving and grooving deductions and 20 LF of the selected rail. Quantities round to two decimals for each end before summation. This app blocks negative widths, negative skew, skew at or above 90°, and invalid numerical results.

Drawing basis: SDBE-1A, Bridge End Pavement with Rail, and the SDBEPR detail families. SDBE-1A matches the 20 ft mid-length, 10 ft side-A minimum, rail width additions and 1 in. bridge-end joint used here. The supplied special drawing PDFs are marked PRELIMINARY / NOT FOR CONSTRUCTION. Other plan sets, including BE-1 and the current SDBE-1, use different arrangements; confirm the applicable project detail. Diagrams show the calculation geometry and section arrangement, not structural design, reinforcement quantities or complete construction details.
